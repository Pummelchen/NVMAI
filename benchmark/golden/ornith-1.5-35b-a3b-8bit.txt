A mutex (mutual exclusion object) is a synchronization primitive used to protect shared resources from being accessed by multiple threads simultaneously. It ensures that only one thread can access the resource at a time, preventing race conditions. You would use a mutex when you need to ensure that a critical section of code, which accesses shared resources, is executed by only one thread at a time.
