A mutex, short for mutual exclusion, is a synchronization primitive used to protect shared resources from being accessed by multiple threads simultaneously. It ensures that only one thread can access the resource at a time, preventing race conditions and data corruption. You would use a mutex when multiple threads need to access a shared resource, such as a file, database, or memory location, to ensure that the resource is accessed safely and consistently.
