# Mutex (Mutual Exclusion Lock)

## What Is a Mutex?

A **mutex** (short for **mutual exclusion**) is a synchronization primitive that ensures only **one thread at a time** can access a shared resource. It acts as a lock: when a thread acquires the mutex, other threads attempting to acquire it are blocked until the lock is released.
